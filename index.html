Good to know

🌡️ Weather

January is usually dry and in the mid-70s °F, with the ocean around 75°F. A cold front can bring a windy, cooler day or two, so we'll swap days if needed.

🚋 Getting around South Beach

The Miami Beach trolley is free and runs about every 20 minutes from 8 AM to 11 PM. The Big Bus covers Wynwood, Downtown and Bayside. Uber for anything else.

📅 Opening days

  • Botanical Garden: closed Mondays
  • Vizcaya: closed Tuesdays
  • Holocaust Memorial: visit in daylight

🧳 Moving day (Jan 3)

Bags ride in the trunk during Vizcaya and Little Havana. Car goes back to MIA by 2–3 PM, then an Uber to the Fifth Hotel.

🤿 Snorkel day

If the forecast on Dec 29–30 looks windy or cold, we cancel by the morning of Dec 31 and switch to Zoo Miami or the Heritage Cruise.

☀️ Beach rhythm

Sightseeing in the mornings, beach from about noon to 4:30 PM when it's warmest, sunsets at South Pointe.
