    Good to know

    Practical notes

    Getting around

    In South Beach the free trolley runs about every 20 minutes, 8 AM–11 PM. The Big Bus covers Wynwood, Downtown and Bayside. Uber for anything else.

    Opening days

    The Botanical Garden is closed Mondays and Vizcaya is closed Tuesdays. Visit the Holocaust Memorial in daylight.

    Moving day

    On Jan 3 the bags stay in the trunk through Vizcaya and Little Havana. The car goes back to MIA by 2–3 PM, then an Uber to the hotel.

    Snorkel decision

    We check the marine forecast Dec 29–30. If it looks rough, we cancel by about 9 AM Dec 31 and switch to Zoo Miami or the no-swim cruise.

    Beach rhythm

    Sightseeing in the mornings, beach from about noon to 4:30 PM when it's warmest, then sunset at South Pointe.

    Departure day

    Check out by 11 AM on Jan 7. The hotel holds bags, so there's time for a last walk before heading to the airport.
