    Getting around

    In South Beach the free trolley runs about every 20 minutes, 8 AM–11 PM. The Big Bus covers Wynwood, Downtown and Bayside. Uber for the rest.

    Opening days

    The Botanical Garden is closed Mondays and Vizcaya is closed Tuesdays. Visit the Holocaust Memorial in daylight.

    Moving day

    On Jan 3 the bags ride in the trunk through Vizcaya and Little Havana. The car goes back to MIA by 2–3 PM, then an Uber to South Beach. Book the car one-way from FLL.

    Snorkel decision

    Check the marine forecast Dec 29–30. If it looks rough, switch by about 9 AM Dec 31, before the 48-hour refund window closes.

    Beach rhythm

    Sightseeing in the mornings, beach from about noon to 4:30 PM, then sunset at South Pointe.

    Departure day

    Check out by 11 AM on Jan 7; the hotel holds bags. Leave for Fort Lauderdale (FLL) by 4 PM for the 7:04 PM flight.
